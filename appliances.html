Kitchen remodel, galley layout with white quartzite counters (3 cm). Notes cover the gotchas and unexpected requirements for this kitchen: 48" aisle (measured between cabinet faces; every aisle figure below uses it), 96" ceiling, island sink + cooktop, and the under-counter oven and the appliance hutch above it in the wall run sketch. Verify every number against the model's installation instructions before cutting anything.

cooktopBoschNIT8061UC (800 Series 30" induction, drop-in)Dedicated 30 A, 208/240 V circuit, hardwired (37" conduit, no plug).
Cutout 28 7/8" W × 20" D; unit is 31" W × 21 1/4" D and drops 4 1/8" below the counter, which is why the base is an ASB36 appliance sink base: its fixed false drawer front leaves that top zone open for the cooktop body.
Base is 36" wide: ~34 1/2" inside clears the 28 7/8" cutout by ~2 13/16" a side. A 30" base (~28 1/2" inside) would have needed its side panels notched.
Depth is the binding constraint: min 2" from cutout to the back wall (the island riser counts as a wall) and 2 1/4" to the counter front. On the 25" island counter that leaves 25 − 2 − 2 1/4 = 20 3/4" for a 20" cutout: only 3/4" of slack.
Needs a 1" air gap below the unit; keep drawer contents clear of it.
Induction needs ferromagnetic cookware: aluminum, copper and most glass won't heat. Test pans with a magnet.

ventilationBoschHIB82651UC (800 Series 42" island box canopy)600 CFM, no make-up air needed. Plano's amendment to IRC M1503.6 allows up to 600 CFM without make-up air when every appliance is sealed-combustion, power-vent or electric, and this house is all-electric. That puts this hood exactly at the limit, so don't upgrade to a stronger blower without revisiting this.
8" round duct must run up through the ceiling over the island. Plan the joist path and roof/wall exit early. A recirculating kit (HIREC5UC) is the fallback.
Hardwired 120 V, no plug.
Min 24" above an electric cooktop. Stock chimney covers 27 7/16"–42 7/8", so on a 96" ceiling the canopy bottom can sit roughly 50.7"–66.2". Set at 66 3/16" (decided 2026-10-13), the top of that range with the chimney at its minimum, 30 7/16" above the 35.75" counter. Order the stock chimney; no extension is needed.
Island hoods hang from the ceiling: add solid blocking between joists before drywall.
