# Appliances
 
Kitchen remodel, galley layout with white quartzite counters (3 cm). Notes cover the gotchas and unexpected requirements for **this** kitchen: 48" aisle, 96" ceiling, island sink + cooktop, and the under-counter oven and the appliance hutch above it in the south run. Verify every number against the model's installation instructions before cutting anything.
