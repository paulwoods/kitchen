Cabinets

index

Every cabinet, panel and filler in the two runs, left to right as drawn in the sketches. Sizes are width × height × depth in inches; elevations are measured from the floor. Base cabinets are 34.5" tall with a 4.5" toe kick, under a 3 cm (1.25") quartzite counter at 35.75". Ceiling is 96".

Colors (decided 2026-10-26; base color changed from Haze to Naval 2026-10-27, and the wall run's base cabinets to Bright White the same day): the island base cabinets, the dishwasher panel and the island plywood ends in Monaco Maple Naval; the wall run's base cabinets and base filler, the wall cabinets, the pantry, the TEPs and the tall fillers in Monaco Maple Bright White (cabinets.com Cordova collection). Hardware on the island base cabinets is gold; on the rest it stays dark (2026-10-27). Drawn in those colors in the front sketches and model.html.

Depths not given by the cabinet code are the usual stock depths (24" base, 12" wall) and are not confirmed.
