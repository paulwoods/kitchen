Chosen 2026-10-25 over the Bosch HIB82651UC.
600 CFM, no make-up air needed. It moves 500 CFM on the 6" duct (2026-11-02). Plano's amendment to IRC M1503.6 allows up to 600 CFM without make-up air when every appliance is sealed-combustion, power-vent or electric, and this house is all-electric.
