Request for a coordinated check

To the cabinet supplier and the countertop fabricator, coordinated with the general contractor:

Before we approve the cabinet order and the countertop layout, please confirm:

  1. The reference points behind each dimension above: cabinet box face, door face or countertop edge.
  2. The finished projections into the aisle: door and drawer front thickness, pulls, and the countertop nosing.
  3. How far handles and open doors reach into the aisle, at the fridge, OVD3393, pantry and dishwasher.
  4. The space each appliance needs in front of it to open fully and be loaded or unloaded.

Then tell us what would have to change to give two people room to work side by side in the aisle, while guests can still walk past and sit at the island. Coordinate any change with the island's overhang support and with the hood staying centered over the cooktop.

Section two: remnant request

Use: retain usable offcuts; use undecided. The candidates on paper are in Leftovers.

To the fabricator (and Go-Granite, if they keep any rights to the slabs):

  1. Who owns the leftover stone after fabrication: us or the shop?
  2. After the three counter pieces and the four backsplash pieces are cut, which pieces can be saved, and what sizes are they?
  3. What does it cost to finish the edges of a saved piece, and to deliver it?
  4. For any piece made into something to handle or carry, such as a small serving piece: how much it weighs, whether its edges are comfortable to hold, whether it needs protective feet, and whether this stone and its sealer or treatment suit that use.

We are not asking for a cutting board for knife work.
