1. The finished requirement

Required working aisle: 56 inches clear between opposing finished countertop edges.
  • Affected aisle: the one aisle of the galley, between the island's aisle-side counter edge (spices through Cabinet A) and the wall run's aisle-side counter edge (Garage through Coffee). It is shown on the floor plan (House sketch) and on the Island top and Wall top. Opposite the fridge, pantry and OVD3393 there is no wall counter, so there the requirement applies from the island counter edge to those fronts. That reading is an assumption; confirm it.
  • Superseded: the older 48" cabinet-face spacing (Selections log, 2026-10-24; Countertop log, 2026-11-05) is superseded for this requirement. So is the 56 1/2" door-face spacing of 2026-11-08. Face spacing is no longer the requirement. It now follows from the counter-edge requirement (section 2).
  • The plan now (2026-11-09): 56" between the counter edges, exactly the requirement, with no margin. The wall behind the wall run stays where it was, 106" from the island's cabinet backs, on the line of the old kitchen wall (Countertop and Walls Decision logs).
