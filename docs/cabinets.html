Every cabinet, panel and filler in the two runs, left to right as drawn in the sketches. Sizes are width × height × depth in inches; elevations are measured from the floor. Base cabinets are 34.5" tall with a toe kick 4.5" tall and 3" deep, under a 3 cm (1.25") quartzite counter at 35.75". Ceiling is 96".

Colors (decided 2026-11-01): the upper cabinets, the pantry, and the REP panels and skin by the fridge are Colonial Maple Alabaster, a soft off-white; everything else (the base cabinets, the dishwasher panel and the island's back wall) is Colonial Maple Haze, soft with hints of gray (cabinets.com Cordova collection, a 2 1/4" five-piece door). Hardware on the island base cabinets is gold; on the rest it stays dark (2026-10-27). Drawn in those colors in the front sketches and model.html; the door's recessed panels are not drawn.

Depths not given by the cabinet code are the usual stock depths (24" base, 12" wall) and are not confirmed.
