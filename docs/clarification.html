Email to the plumbing contractor: trench

Subject: Request for formal quote: kitchen floor trench

Hello,

Thank you for the preliminary estimate and our discussion. Please send a formal written quote for the trench in the kitchen slab. So that the quote matches what will be built, please address each item below.

  1. Length and dimensions. We have discussed both 60" and 16'. Please state the saw-cut length (the length of slab cut) separately from the trench dimensions (length, width and depth of the excavation), and say which length you have priced.
  2. Final quantity after demolition. The final length may change once the floor is open. Please explain how the final quantity will be measured, the unit rate for any added or removed length, and how a price change will be agreed in writing before that work goes ahead. If demolition reveals conditions that change the trench length, depth, concrete repair, or related work, please pause and provide the revised quantity and price for written approval before proceeding.
  3. Backfill. Material and method. Who does this work: you, a subcontractor or the homeowner?
  4. Compaction. Method and how it is checked. Who does this work: you, a subcontractor or the homeowner?
  5. Concrete replacement. Thickness, reinforcement or doweling to the existing slab, vapor barrier, and the finish the patch is left at (level and flush with the slab). Who does this work: you, a subcontractor or the homeowner?
  6. Exclusions. Please list everything the quote does not include.
  7. Finish flooring. The homeowner will replace the finish flooring separately; it is not part of this quote. Please state the condition the slab will be left in for the flooring.
  8. Tests and inspections. Please identify the tests (for example, the drain line test) and the inspections required before the trench is covered, who arranges each with the local authority, and who holds the permit.

Please identify each item as included, excluded, or requiring confirmation before work is authorized. No work outside the quoted scope should proceed without written approval.

These items come from preliminary estimates and verbal discussions. Scope, responsibilities and dimensions will be confirmed in your written quote before work begins.

Thank you,

  • Permits and inspections: please check which permits and inspections apply to this work, and who obtains and schedules them.
  • Exclusions: please list everything the quote does not include.

Please identify each item as included, excluded, or requiring confirmation before work is authorized. No work outside the quoted scope should proceed without written approval.
