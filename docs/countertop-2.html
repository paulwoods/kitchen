Ebano at Capstone

What-if sheet, 2026-11-10: the cutting map redone for two book-matched slabs of 132" × 79" instead of the planned 126" × 63": the Ebano quartzite at Capstone, with 1" burned off every side in case the edges are chipped or cracked, leaving 130" × 77" of each. Same pieces as the Countertop page: the cooktop 85" × 44 1/2", the sink 109" × 30 1/2", the wall 84" × 25" and the backsplash, here one 84" × 18 1/4" piece instead of the four 21" pieces planned there. Added here: a 76" × 22" bathroom vanity top from the band above the cooktop piece. Nothing here is decided.

Cutting map

Book-matched slabs open like a book: the second is the mirror of the first across the edge they share (dashed blue). The cooktop piece sits at the bottom right of the first slab and the sink piece at the bottom left of the second, so their ends meet at that edge and the veins mirror across the island seam between plates and trash. Both lose the same 1" there, so they still mirror. Both pieces keep their front (aisle) edge along the slab's bottom edge. The wall piece and the backsplash share the second slab, the backsplash directly above the counter it stands on, so the veins run on up it. The backgrounds are photos of the two slabs, the pieces clear and the offcuts faded. To scale; the trim (pink) comes off first. No kerf or flaws allowed for.

One piece means no backsplash seams. It is 84" long and thin, so it needs careful handling. Its four receptacle cutouts are drawn 2 1/4" × 4" (single-gang boxes, size assumed), centered over each 21" cabinet and 10" below its top edge, at 44" off the floor.
