Backsplash

Stone backsplash along the wall run's whole 114" of counter, above the oven B33, Toaster MWB27, Air Fryer DB27-3 and Coffee DB27-3: 18 1/4" tall, from the 35.75" counter to the bottom of the W3327 and W2727s at 54". That is 14.4 square feet.

Cut as four pieces, 33" × 18 1/4" over the oven and three 27" × 18 1/4", one per cabinet, with the seams on the cabinet dividers so they read as intentional. Each piece has a cutout for its receptacle, centered at 44". All four come out of the first slab, so no third slab is needed.

One 81" piece was ruled out: it would have to come from the 85" × 18 1/2" band above the cooktop piece, leaving 1/4" of height for kerf and edge trim, and quartzite slabs often arrive under their nominal size. The 33" piece has the same 1/4" to spare. If the band comes up short, all four pieces fit the 41" × 63" offcut turned sideways, in two columns (33" and 27", 27" and 27"), but their veins would then run the other way.

Unanswered questions

Nothing below has been sent to a supplier or fabricator, and nothing has been answered.

  1. Care and wear: does this stone need sealing, how often and with what? Does it stain or etch? Stones sold as quartzite vary, so has this one been tested?
  2. Slab approval: photos and approval of the actual slabs (the listing photo is only representative), and an etch and stain test on a sample of the exact stone.
  3. Availability and price: two matching slabs that yield 85" × 44 1/2", 109" × 30 1/2" and 114" × 25" in 3 cm — delivered price, and fabricated and installed price.
  4. Layout: the fabricator's own layout on the 126" × 63" slab, with seam placement and vein matching; is two slabs right?
  5. Fabricator: will The Stumble Stone fabricate and install slabs bought from Go-Granite, and who handles freight and storage? Its website doesn't mention customer-supplied slabs.
  6. Fabrication: can they fabricate and install 3 cm with no subtop, with an eased edge, the island seam, the cutouts above, the dishwasher gap, and a 15" seating overhang on the bracket layout above?
  7. Support: have the fabricator approve the bracket layout for 3 cm quartzite, especially around the cooktop cutout, allowing for people leaning on the seating overhang. Have a contractor or engineer confirm the back wall itself — it is short with nothing tying it to the ceiling, so its anchoring sets the real limit.
  8. Backsplash yield: can the fabricator get the four backsplash pieces (33" and three 27", all 18 1/4" tall) out of the two slabs on their own layout, around veining and flaws, and how do they want the seams and the vein match handled? The offcuts above assume clean rectangular nesting, and the 33" piece has only 1/4" to spare.
  9. Leftovers: ask for the remnants in writing, since fabricators often keep them unless the contract says otherwise.
  10. Support at the dishwasher gap, the sink and cooktop cutouts and the sink base with no subtop: confirm with the fabricator.
  11. Receptacles behind spices and plates, centered at 29", face the seats — check them against knees and plugs.
  12. Finish test (countertop.md): still pending. No samples in hand; stone and cabinet samples not yet compared in daytime, evening, 3000K, 3500K and 4000K.
