Backsplash

Stone backsplash along the wall run's whole 84" of counter, above the Garage B21-FH and the Toaster, Air Fryer and Coffee DB21-3s: 18 1/4" tall, from the 35.75" counter to the bottom of the W2127s at 54". That is 10.6 square feet.

Cut as four pieces, 21" × 18 1/4", one per cabinet, with the seams on the cabinet dividers so they read as intentional. Each piece has a cutout for its receptacle, centered at 44". Three come out of the first slab and one out of the second, all with the same grain direction, so no third slab is needed. Turned 90°, all four would fit on the first slab, if the fabricator prefers one slab to one grain direction.

Unanswered questions

Nothing below has been sent to a supplier or fabricator, and nothing has been answered.

  1. Care and wear: does this stone need sealing, how often and with what? Does it stain or etch? Stones sold as quartzite vary, so has this one been tested?
  2. Slab approval: photos and approval of the actual slabs (the listing photo is only representative), and an etch and stain test on a sample of the exact stone.
  3. Availability and price: two matching slabs that yield 85" × 44 1/2", 109" × 30 1/2" and 84" × 25" in 3 cm — delivered price, and fabricated and installed price.
  4. Layout: the fabricator's own layout on the 126" × 63" slab, with seam placement and vein matching; is two slabs right?
  5. Fabricator: will The Stumble Stone fabricate and install slabs bought from Go-Granite, and who handles freight and storage? Its website doesn't mention customer-supplied slabs.
  6. Fabrication: can they fabricate and install 3 cm with no subtop, with an eased edge, the island seam, the cutouts above, the dishwasher gap, and a 15" seating overhang on the bracket layout above?
  7. Support: have the fabricator approve the bracket layout for 3 cm quartzite, especially around the cooktop cutout, allowing for people leaning on the seating overhang. Have an engineer confirm the back wall itself — it is short with nothing tying it to the ceiling, so its anchoring sets the real limit.
  8. Backsplash yield: can the fabricator get the four backsplash pieces (21" × 18 1/4") out of the two slabs on their own layout, around veining and flaws, and how do they want the seams and the vein match handled? The offcuts above assume clean rectangular nesting.
  9. Leftovers: ask for the remnants in writing, since fabricators often keep them unless the contract says otherwise.
  10. Support at the dishwasher gap, the sink and cooktop cutouts and the sink base with no subtop: confirm with the fabricator.
  11. Receptacles behind spices and plates, centered at 29", face the seats — check them against knees and plugs.
  12. Finish test (countertop.md): still pending. No samples in hand; stone and cabinet samples not yet compared in daytime, evening, 3000K, 3500K and 4000K.
