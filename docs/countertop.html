Unanswered questions

Nothing below has been sent to a supplier or fabricator, and nothing has been answered.

  1. Care and wear: does this stone need sealing, how often and with what? Does it stain or etch? Stones sold as quartzite vary, so has this one been tested?
  2. Slab approval: photos and approval of the actual slabs (the listing photo is only representative), and an etch and stain test on a sample of the exact stone.
  3. Availability and price: two matching slabs that yield 85" × 45 1/4", 109" × 31 1/4" and 84" × 25 3/4" in 3 cm — delivered price, and fabricated and installed price.
  4. Layout: the fabricator's own layout on the 126" × 63" slab, with seam placement and vein matching; is two slabs right?
  5. Fabricator: will The Stumble Stone fabricate and install slabs bought from Go-Granite, and who handles freight and storage? Its website doesn't mention customer-supplied slabs.
  6. Fabrication: can they fabricate and install 3 cm with no subtop, with an eased edge, the island seam, the cutouts above, the dishwasher gap, and a 15" seating overhang on the bracket layout above?
  7. Support: have the fabricator approve the bracket layout for 3 cm quartzite, especially around the cooktop cutout, allowing for people leaning on the seating overhang. Have an engineer confirm the back wall itself — it is short with nothing tying it to the ceiling, so its anchoring sets the real limit.

The brief

The exercise the brief sections above answer (2026-11-04).

Define the Details to Verify. Spend about 10 minutes with paper or a document. No purchases, supplier replies, or new measurements are required; use TBD wherever information is missing.

Write the Proposed Specification. Start with leading candidate: Luce di Luna quartzite; alternative: soapstone; proposed thickness: 3 centimeters, carried from your existing pages. Add finish and edge as proposed or TBD. Identify actual-slab inspection, material identification, care guidance, and sample comparison as pending unless you now have evidence.

Map the Fabrication Decisions. Sketch two rectangles representing the galley's counter runs. Label prep, sink, cooktop, and landing areas, then mark any proposed seams, exposed edges, backsplash, overhangs, seating, or bookshelf interfaces. Label unknown dimensions TBD and note that the fabricator must establish the actual seam and support proposal.

Prepare Three Focused Questions.

  1. For the actual slab and proposed thickness, what edge, cutout, finish, and care details do you recommend, and what documentation supports them?
  2. Can you provide a proposed seam and veining layout based on slab size, cutouts, transport, and installation access?
  3. What cabinet preparation and support are required at spans, cutouts, and overhangs, and who coordinates approval with the cabinet supplier?

Protect the Working Kitchen. Add a note requiring finished projections and supports to preserve your selected 48-inch working aisle, appliance access, and guest connection. Keep island depth, seating, and bookshelf details provisional wherever they remain unresolved. For each question, record an owner, evidence requested, and actual status: drafted, sent, or answered.
