Unanswered questions

Nothing below has been sent to a supplier or fabricator, and nothing has been answered.

  1. Care and wear: does this stone need sealing, how often and with what? Does it stain or etch? Stones sold as quartzite vary, so has this one been tested?
  2. Slab approval: photos and approval of the actual slabs (the listing photo is only representative), and an etch and stain test on a sample of the exact stone.
  3. Availability and price: two matching slabs that yield 85" × 44 1/2", 109" × 30 1/2" and 114" × 25" in 3 cm — delivered price, and fabricated and installed price.
  4. Layout: the fabricator's own layout on the 126" × 63" slab, with seam placement and vein matching; is two slabs right?
  5. Fabrication: can they fabricate and install 3 cm with no subtop, with the island seam, the cutouts above, the dishwasher gap, and a 15" seating overhang on the bracket layout above?
  6. Support: have the fabricator approve the bracket layout for 3 cm quartzite, especially around the cooktop cutout, allowing for people leaning on the seating overhang. Have a contractor or engineer confirm the back wall itself — it is short with nothing tying it to the ceiling, so its anchoring sets the real limit.
  7. Backsplash yield: can the fabricator get the three 27" × 18 1/4" backsplash pieces out of the two slabs on their own layout, around veining and flaws, and how do they want the seams and the vein match handled? The 41" × 63" offcut above assumes clean rectangular nesting.
  8. Edge profile: which edges does the fabricator offer, and which is chosen? Not decided.
  9. Support at the dishwasher gap, the sink and cooktop cutouts and the sink base with no subtop: confirm with the fabricator.
  10. Receptacles behind spices and plates, centered at 29", face the seats — check them against knees and plugs.
  11. Finish test (countertop.md): still pending. No samples in hand; stone and cabinet samples not yet compared in daytime, evening, 3000K, 3500K and 4000K.
