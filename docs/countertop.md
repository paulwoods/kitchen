## The brief

Spend about 10 minutes with paper or a document. Physical samples are helpful but not required; do not purchase anything or wait for supplier replies to complete this planning exercise.

**Compare Two Candidates.** Make two rows: preferred soapstone and either granite or quartzite as a comparison candidate. For each, record: heat and scratch considerations; sealing or appearance-care questions; whether visible wear is acceptable to you; and the slab-specific evidence still needed. If a product is not selected, write "candidate TBD." Do not invent prices or care instructions.

**Plan the Finish Test.** List the samples needed: representative soapstone, Colonial Maple Haze, and Colonial Maple Alabaster. View stone horizontally and cabinet samples vertically, as they will be installed. Compare them together in daytime and evening conditions; where suitable lighting is available, compare 3000K, 3500K, and 4000K while keeping sample positions unchanged. Record actual conditions used, or mark this test pending. A screen image is inspiration, not final color approval.

**Name the Decision and Evidence.** Finish with a provisional preference and the reason it supports your everyday prep, cleanup, and social use. Write three questions for a stone supplier or fabricator: care and wear for the exact stone; availability and pricing for the actual scope; and fabrication constraints affecting edges, cutouts, seams, or support. Keep your existing electrician and Proline questions separate, with their actual status unchanged unless you have new information.
