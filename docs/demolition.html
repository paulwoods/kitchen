Dumpster

Plano's rule

Plano requires every hauler that takes construction and demolition debris out of the city to hold a city permit. A hauler without one gets a stop work order and a criminal citation on the job, with fines of up to $2,000 a day (Permitted Recycling Haulers). The building permit application also asks who the trash hauler is.

Source separation is required. Only Republic Services accepts commingled loads. A kitchen tear-out mixes everything: cabinets, drywall, counters, old appliances, metal. To use any other hauler on the list, the debris has to be sorted by material (metal; drywall and wood; stone, tile and concrete).

It applies to this remodel. The city's residential Bulky Waste Accepted Items page excludes contractor waste from bulky waste and special paid collection: "Waste generated from projects requiring the use of a contractor will need to be disposed of by the contractor or by contracting waste through a permitted hauler. Waste must be disposed of directly at the 121 Regional Disposal Facility." This job uses contractors (the beam, electrical, plumbing), so a dumpster we rent has to come from the list below.

The old stove and microwave can still go out as bulky waste, and the old fridge as a special paid collection, which keeps their weight out of the dumpster. The city's pages don't say whether the owner may take the debris to the 121 Regional Disposal Facility without a hauler. Commercial Waste & Recycling: 972-769-4393.

Recommended

  1. Republic Services, 4200 E 14th St, Plano, TX 75074 · 972-422-2341. The only permitted hauler that takes a mixed load, and its yard is in Plano. It posts no 20-yard price, so call for a quote.
  2. Fill N Go Waste, 1500 Capital Dr, Carrollton · 833-345-5646, or Precision Demolition, 1125 E State Hwy 121, Lewisville · 972-434-0800. Nearby, for a second quote, but only if the debris is sorted.

Budget Dumpster, Dumpsters.com, Zters, Trash Daddy and Bin There Dump That all advertise in Plano, but none is on the city's list. Ask any of them whether a permitted hauler (a "contracted transporter") does the hauling before booking.

Size and price

A 20-yard roll-off (about 22' × 7 1/2' × 4 1/2') holds one kitchen tear-out. Advertised Plano prices for a 20-yard run about $555–$700 for 7 days with about 2 tons included (Zters, ASAP Marketplace, Dumpsters.com). The Price page carries it as TBD, estimated at $600.

When getting a quote, ask:

  • How many tons are included, and the charge per ton over that, often about $100. The old counters are heavy if they're stone.
  • How many days are included, and the charge per extra day.
  • Whether delivery and pickup are included.
  • What it won't take: paint, refrigerant (the old fridge), batteries, tires.
  • Driveway placement, on boards to protect the concrete. A container in the street may need a city permit.
