Working basis

Open galley for two users; required working aisle: 56 inches clear between opposing finished countertop edges.
  • Coordination reference: the current floor plan, sheet "House" in drawings/sketches.html (row 1.1). Every other document is checked against it.
  • Cabinet-face spacing is derived, not set: face spacing = 56" + the island's aisle-side countertop projection past its faces + the wall run's aisle-side countertop projection past its faces. Both projections are the actual ones, from the cabinet supplier (front thickness) and the fabricator (finished edge). Neither is confirmed, so no face spacing is stated here. The plan's working values are in Aisle Revision Note, section 2, marked to be confirmed.
  • To settle with those projections: the documents don't yet agree on the front thickness. kitchen.js (DIMS.front, owner, 2026-11-09) and the Cabinets item under Decisions, before the cabinets are ordered (item 2) give different values.

Coverage is not approval

All six groups have a document. None is approved, and the missing rows above are not made here. Each provisional detail stays with its own review: the structure with the engineer, the countertop support, subtop and seam with the fabricator and the cabinet supplier, the boxes and circuits with the electrician, and the permit questions with the city. Until those are answered in writing and recorded in each page's Decision log, the package stays "Design coordination—not released for construction".
