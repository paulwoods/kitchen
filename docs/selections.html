Kitchen remodel, galley layout with Luce di Luna quartzite counters (3 cm). Notes cover the gotchas and unexpected requirements for this kitchen: 48" aisle (measured between the door and drawer faces, 3/4" in front of the cabinet boxes; every aisle figure below uses it), 96" ceiling, island sink + cooktop, and the combination oven in the tall OVD3393 in the wall run sketch. Verify every number against the model's installation instructions before cutting anything.
