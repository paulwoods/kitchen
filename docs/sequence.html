Sequence

Draft order of work from demolition to move-in, with the owner as general contractor, 2026-11-04 · index

Written from the plan, not from a site visit. Nothing records the existing kitchen, so the demolition steps are general until the room is measured (Walls, Open). No trade has seen or bid this. The days are rough working-day estimates, not quotes, and exclude waiting on orders, permits and inspectors: about 7 to 11 weeks on site once everything is bought.

What can stop the job

  • Open items. Nothing is ordered or cut while any page has items under Open (the count is on the index).
  • The beam. A load bearing wall stands where the island goes. Nobody removes it until the engineer has designed the beam and the permit is in hand (steps 2 and 4), and it stays shored until the beam carries the load (steps 11 to 13).
  • The slab. Find out whether it is post-tensioned before anything cuts or anchors into it (step 2). A cut cable can injure someone and damage the slab. This covers the 60" trench, the island wall's anchors and any footings for the beam's posts.
  • The floor. It isn't chosen, and it sets when the floor goes in and the height everything measures from (step 25).
  • The island wall. Its anchoring and the seven brackets need approval before it is framed, because the channels are cut in its top plate (steps 2 and 17).
  • Stone template. It can't happen until the base cabinets are set and the sink, cooktop and faucet are on site. The kitchen has no sink or counters from demolition until the stone is in.
