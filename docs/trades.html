Load bearing wall contractors

Researched 2026-11-09; moved here from Demolition 2026-11-12.

RankCompanyStrengthsWatch for
1Load Bearing Wall Pros (Plano)In business since 2015, with the most reviews (Google about 4.9 from roughly 410, per one listing). An in-house engineer stamps the drawings. Lifetime structural warranty you can pass to a buyer, $2M liability policy. Published price range of $3,500–$10,000.You pull the permit yourself. Its listings give different addresses and review counts.
2DFW Wall Removal (Carrollton)4.9★ from 196 Google reviews. A licensed engineer signs off on every beam, and they pull the permit and schedule the inspection. 12-year transferable warranty, $1M insurance.Only in business since 2022. No address on its website.
3Load Bearing Wall Experts (11550 Plano Rd #124, Dallas)4.7★ from 44+ Google reviews. They handle the permit and the engineering.The workmanship guarantee doesn't say how long it lasts. The site doesn't mention its years in business.
AlternateLoad Bearing Beam Masters (McKinney)5.0★ on HomeAdvisor, which also screens its contractors. One review describes a kitchen-to-living-room wall removal.Only about 6 reviews, and no Google or BBB pages found.

Pick

Get quotes from #1 and #2. DFW Wall Removal does the most work for you, since it pulls the permit. Load Bearing Wall Pros has the longer history and the better warranty.

Ask for with each quote

  • The stamped engineer's drawing (beam size, posts, footings)
  • Who pulls the permit
  • A certificate of insurance with your name on it
  • The warranty in writing

Countertop fabricators

Researched 2026-11-09. For the countertop: template, fabricate and install our own Luce di Luna quartzite slabs from Go-Granite. None of these shops says on its site that it takes customer-supplied slabs or does a full turnkey job (sink mount, cutouts, backsplash), so ask each one.

RankCompanyStrengthsWatch for
1Granite Republic (Dallas)The most reviews (Birdeye 5★ from 178). Fabricates and installs granite, quartz, marble, porcelain and quartzite. Reviewers praise communication, installation and prices; Houzz lists typical jobs at $2,700–$7,000.Listings disagree on the founding year (2005 or 2016) and the address. No published warranty, and in-house fabrication isn't confirmed.
2The Stumble Stone (Dallas / Arlington)Already the tentative pick on the Countertop page. 5.0★ from 74 Thumbtack reviews; the owner is praised for responsiveness. Lists quartzite.Founded in 2022. Most reviews are on Thumbtack. Addresses differ between listings.
3DFW Granite (10230 Bickham Rd #300, Dallas)In business since 1994. Google 5, Yelp 4.9 (47 reviews). Its own install crews; a reviewer had a Taj Mahal quartzite kitchen done.A mystery shopper found staff unfriendly and calls unanswered. 70% deposit, and a 1-year workmanship warranty.
AlternateStonemode (2840 Reward Ln, Dallas)Family owned, works for designers and builders. A reviewer had Taj Mahal quartzite installed and the remnants used for two bathrooms.Only 6 Yelp reviews (4.2), and the quartzite review is from 2017.

Pick

Get quotes from #1 and #2. Granite Republic has the longest review record; The Stumble Stone is already on the plan.

Ask for with each quote

  • Whether they fabricate customer-supplied slabs, and who takes the risk if a slab cracks
  • That the price covers template, fabrication, delivery, install, sink mounting, the cooktop and sink cutouts and the faucet and air button holes
  • Whether they follow the fabrication map (seams, grain direction) and who keeps the remnants
  • Experience with quartzite, and a recent quartzite job to see
  • The warranty and the deposit in writing
