Everyone the job hires: the work, what to send them, what to ask, the companies to choose from and where the bid stands, gathered 2026-11-12 · index

The owner is the general contractor (Sequence, 2026-11-04) and does the framing, drywall, paint and scribing. Only the city and Go-Granite (slab, fabrication and installation, 2026-11-16) have been written to. The steps are the Sequence's, the price lines the Price page's (all TBD until there's a bid). A question list stays on the page it was written for and is linked here. Get each trade's license and insurance certificate with its bid (step 4).

Each trade below has four companies to choose from, the best fit first; the one hired gets ✅ and bold. To make the list a company needs (2026-11-23): an active state license where the trade has one, a Google rating of 4.5★ or better from at least 25 reviews, at least 5 years in business (founded 2021 or earlier) and an office within about 20 miles of Plano 75025. Where fewer than four pass, the nearest misses fill the table and their notes say which bar they miss. Licenses are read from the state's own lists (TDLR's open data of 2026-09-18, TSBPE's master plumber list, TBPELS's firm roster); years from the company, BBB or the Texas Comptroller's franchise tax records (the date the company was formed in Texas); ratings from Google Maps on the date given. Check the license again before signing.
