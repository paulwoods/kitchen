Island wall

A short wall along the back of the island cabinets, 2x4 studs with 1/2" drywall on both sides and both ends. The back and ends are painted Colonial Maple Haze (assumed). A load bearing wall stands where the island goes now and comes out first, with a beam in its place (Issues).

Build

  • Height: a bottom plate and a double top plate take 4 1/2" of the 34.5", leaving 30" studs (worked out from 1 1/2" plates; check against the floor and the plate material).
  • Studs: one under each bracket where the boxes allow. Set the studs around the boxes near 12", 27" and 72" from the island's left end.
  • Anchoring: to the slab and tied to the cabinets, as the engineer specifies. The wall has nothing tying it to the ceiling, so this sets the limit for the seating overhang. Scan the slab first, since it may be post-tensioned.
  • Overhang: the counter runs 15" past the wall behind spices, cooktop and plates (the first 84"), and 1" past it along the rest. Three stools sit under the 15" stretch.
  • Brackets: seven Iron Supports 16" flat brackets, 2.5" × 1/2" steel, each in a 1/2" channel cut in the top plate before framing. They start at the studs' cabinet side and stop 3" short of the counter's edge. Spaced evenly, they fall about 4", 17", 29", 42", 55", 67" and 80" from the left end. The layout isn't approved.
  • Utilities: the electrical conduit comes up from the 60" slab trench inside the wall's line. The sink's drain and vent also run in the wall, clear of the receptacle box at 123".

Side wall right of the wall cabinets

A stud wall at the Coffee end, 2x4 studs with 1/2" drywall on both sides. It starts at the room wall and comes out 24", the depth of the cabinets. There is no matching wall at the fridge end, which the FP2496 skin finishes.

  • Plumb: frame it dead plumb. Coffee, its W2127 and W2112 butt it, and the 84" to the OVD3393 is measured from it. The owner scribes Coffee and its uppers to it (Issues, 2026-11-02).
  • Cabinet face: the counter butts it with no overhang. The riser molding across the uppers ends at it, 93" to 96".
  • Outside face: a 4-gang box with the four Lutron Caséta PD-5NE dimmers, centered on the 24" at 48" (height assumed), left to right A, T, X, UC. Neutral required. The four Pico remotes are on the FP2496 skin at the other end.

Still to settle

  • Island wall anchoring and the bracket layout need the engineer's and the fabricator's approval before framing, since the channels are cut in the top plate (Issues).
  • The third bracket, about 29" from the left end, lands at the edge of the cooktop junction box (25" to 29" if it is 4" wide, centered at 27"), so the stud under it can't go straight down. Move the stud or the box.
  • The room wall's construction and the room's length have to be measured. With the side wall, the run takes 197 3/4".
  • The counter is 25" deep and the side wall 24", so the counter's front edge runs 1" past the wall's end. Decide whether to accept that or make the wall 25". The backsplash page's idea of a 25" stone piece on this wall would pass its end by the same 1".
  • Paint for the side wall and the patched room wall isn't chosen.
  • Framing the side wall isn't on the Price page, and whether the owner or a trade frames, hangs and paints isn't decided.
