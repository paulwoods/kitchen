Island wall

A short wall along the back of the island cabinets, 2x4 studs with 1/2" drywall on both sides and both ends. The back and ends are painted Colonial Maple Haze (assumed). A load bearing wall stands where the island goes now and comes out first, with a beam in its place (Open).

Build

  • Height: a bottom plate and a double top plate take 4 1/2" of the 34 1/2", leaving 30" studs (worked out from 1 1/2" plates; check against the floor and the plate material).
  • Studs: one under each bracket where the boxes allow. Set the studs around the boxes near 12", 27" and 72" from the island's left end.
  • Anchoring: to the slab and tied to the cabinets, as the engineer specifies. The wall has nothing tying it to the ceiling, so this sets the limit for the seating overhang. Scan the slab first, since it may be post-tensioned (look for a stamp or tag near the garage or front door; drill nothing until the cables are found), and for radiant tubing, plumbing and conduit.
    • Bottom plate: pressure-treated 2x4 (required on concrete, IRC R317.1), on foam sill seal with construction adhesive under it.
    • Fasteners (a starting point until the engineer sets them): powder-actuated pins 2 1/2"–3" with washers, or 3/16" × 2 3/4" concrete screws in hammer-drilled holes. One within 6" of each end, then every 12"–16" (closer than the usual 24", since the wall stands free), at least 2" from the slab's edge and control joints, clear of the conduit trench.
    • Cabinets screwed to the studs and to each other; with the countertop on, the island acts as one piece.
  • Overhang: the counter runs 15" past the wall behind spices, cooktop and plates (the first 84"), and 1" past it along the rest. Three stools sit under the 15" stretch.
  • Brackets: seven Iron Supports 16" flat brackets, 2.5" × 1/2" steel, each in a 1/2" channel cut in the top plate before framing. They start at the studs' cabinet side and stop 3" short of the counter's edge. Spaced evenly, they fall about 4", 17", 29", 42", 55", 67" and 80" from the left end. The layout isn't approved.
  • Utilities: the electrical conduit comes up from the 60" slab trench inside the wall's line. The sink's drain and vent also run in the wall, clear of the receptacle box at 123". The existing drain and hot and cold supplies are 170" from the wall of the house (measured 2026-11-08), 116 1/2" from the island's left end; whether they stay there is open.

Room wall behind the wall cabinets

Nothing records this wall: its thickness, what is in it, or whether it is exterior or carries load. The plan assumes it stays, apart from the three changes below. Open it where the work below needs it and patch it afterward. It has to be flat where cabinets land.

Measured from the wall of the house, this line is the 65" wall (0" to 65"), a 41" doorway (65" to 106") and the 136" wall (106" to 242").

  • Close the doorway: frame it in and drywall it to match the wall. Air Fryer and Toaster back onto it, so the Air Fryer receptacle (85" from the wall of the house) and the backing for their upper cabinets go in the new framing. The doorway's edges fall at two receptacles: Coffee's at 64" and Toaster's at 106" are on the jambs at 65" and 106", so the framing there has to leave room for those boxes, or they shift.
  • Open the 65" wall: take it out from the wall of the house to the side wall's outer face, a 49" opening. Its last 16" stays, behind the side wall and the Coffee end of the run. Whether it carries load isn't recorded: the owner has it inspected by a load bearing wall company along with the other walls, and any header follows from that (Sequence, step 2).
  • Remove the two 38" closet stubs, about 106" and 149" from the wall of the house. They stick out from the 136" wall into the Toaster and Oven cabinets' space.

Side wall right of the wall cabinets

A stud wall at the Coffee end, 2x4 studs with 1/2" drywall on both sides. It starts at the room wall and comes out 24", the depth of the cabinets. There is no matching wall at the fridge end, which the FP2496 skin finishes.

  • Plumb: frame it dead plumb. Coffee, its W2127 and W2112 butt it, and the 84" to the OVD3393 is measured from it. The owner scribes Coffee and its uppers to it (Decision log, 2026-11-02).
  • Cabinet face: the counter butts it with no overhang. The riser molding across the uppers ends at it, 93" to 96".
  • Outside face: a 4-gang box with the four Lutron Caséta PD-5NE dimmers, centered on the 24" at 48" (height assumed), left to right ambient, task, accent, under-cabinet. Neutral required. The four Pico remotes are on the FP2496 skin at the other end.

Decide whether to accept that or make the wall 25". A 25" stone backsplash on this wall would pass its end by the same 1".
