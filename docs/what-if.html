Cutting map

Book-matched slabs open like a book: the second is the mirror of the first across the edge they share (dashed blue). The cooktop piece sits at the bottom right of the first slab and the sink piece at the bottom left of the second, so their ends meet at that edge and the veins mirror across the island seam between plates and trash. Both lose the same 1" there, so they still mirror. Both pieces keep their front (aisle) edge along the slab's bottom edge. The wall piece and the backsplash share the second slab, the backsplash directly above the counter it stands on, so the veins run on up it. The backgrounds are photos of the two slabs, the pieces clear and the offcuts faded. To scale; the trim (pink) comes off first, then the saw cuts in the numbered order. No kerf or flaws allowed for.
