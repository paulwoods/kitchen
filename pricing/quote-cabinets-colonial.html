Construction

What sets it apart: the same Cordova box, drawers and Blum hardware as Monaco, with a five-piece door in solid maple, factory painted: Haze, soft with hints of gray, on the base cabinets and dishwasher panel; Alabaster, a soft off-white, on the pantry, oven cabinet, upper cabinets and fridge panels. The two cost the same. Exposed sides cost extra.

  • Type: frameless (full access), shipped assembled. All Cordova cabinets are made and assembled in the USA.
  • Box: 5/8" plywood sides, top and bottom, UV finished, natural maple interior with edgebanding to match.
  • Back: 1/4" UV finished birch, with 5/8" plywood hanging rails and stretchers (7/8" at the rails).
  • Doors and drawer fronts (Colonial): 2 1/4" solid Northern hard rock maple stiles and rails, cope and stick, 3/16" MDF recessed center panel, subtle bevel detail; 3/4" solid maple drawer fronts.
  • Shelves: 3/4" plywood, adjustable; half depth in base cabinets, full depth in wall cabinets.
  • Drawer boxes: 5/8" solid maple, English dovetail, 1/4" plywood bottom captured on all four sides.
  • Drawer sizes inside (cabinets.com interior drawer box sheet, frameless): 19 3/4" deep; 18 1/8" wide in a 21" base (assumed, same margin; the sheet was not re-read), 21 1/8" in a 24", 30 1/8" in a 33", 33 1/8" in a 36". In a 3-drawer base the top box is 3 1/4" tall and the two below are 5 1/4" (side heights as read off the sheet). The 4.3" spice jars do not stand upright in the top drawer.
  • Glides: Blum full-extension undermount with integrated soft close (Blum Motion).
  • Hinges: Blum, integrated soft close, 6-way cam adjustable.
  • Exposed sides: unfinished. "Matching Finished Sides" is a paid option on each cabinet, both sides only: 10% of a base cabinet's price, 15% of a wall cabinet's (read 2026-11-01), 10% of the pantry's (read 2026-11-02), 20% of the OVD3393's (read 2026-11-04). It is priced on the three cabinets with an exposed side.
  • Toe kick and legs: toe kick covers are sold separately: TK8 for frameless cabinets, 1/4" veneered MDF finished on one side, 96" × 4.375", $48.76, sale $31.69 (read 2026-11-02). They are priced on the Price page, not in this quote. Legs are not stated.
  • Riser molding: sold separately: SSM-4.50, plywood core, veneered and finished to match on the face and one long edge, 96" × 4 1/2" × 3/4", $94.19, sale $61.22 (read 2026-11-03). It is priced on the Price page, not in this quote.
  • Certifications (site-wide): CARB 2 compliant, KCMA quality tested.
  • Warranty: not stated on the product or collection pages.
