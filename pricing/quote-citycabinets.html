Construction

What sets it apart: City looks like a sister line to Barker (its site links to Barker's sites and shares its page layouts; not stated outright), with a similar built-to-size approach (widths and heights in 1/4" steps, depths in 1" steps) and the same frameless, flat-packed design. Compared with Barker it has a birch box instead of maple and rubberwood drawers instead of hard maple. The fronts arrive raw, ready to paint on site, and exposed sides need separate end panels.

  • Type: frameless, full overlay (European style), ready to assemble with confirmat screws. Construction holes show on the sides, top and bottom once assembled.
  • Box: 3/4" plywood (11, 9 or 7 ply, depending on availability), real birch veneer with a baked-on UV clear finish, CARB2 compliant or better. Cut in Oregon from globally sourced material. Glass door cabinets have a raw interior in the door's wood instead, to finish on site.
  • Back: not stated separately. A 36" wide tall cabinet's back comes in two pieces; the W2D's listing doesn't say.
  • Doors and drawer fronts (as quoted): slab plywood, paint grade: real wood veneer over plywood with banded edges, vertical grain, not grain matched, sanded and raw. City's terms warn that grain and small defects will show through paint, and that seams at the banded edges are not covered by the warranty; they recommend a full painted or stained door instead. Standard drawer fronts: 5 7/8" top drawer, the lower drawers equal.
  • Glass doors: priced with the Shaker Inset Panel frame, as a glass door needs a frame (the W1DGLASS page also lists slab plywood at +$30, which doesn't make sense with glass; ask). The glass is not included.
  • Shelves: not stated. Roll-outs are rubberwood.
  • Drawer boxes: 5/8" solid rubberwood, dovetailed, 1/4" bottom, clearcoated in a matte sheen, shipped assembled; the dovetail joints are left raw.
  • Drawer sizes inside: not published. The cooktop base lets you pick the drawer box depth (12" to 21"); 21" is priced, to clear the cooktop body.
  • Glides: Blum undermount, soft close, full extension. The product pages say Tandem at 100 lb; the home page says Movento at 125 lb. Ask which ships.
  • Hinges: Blum Clip Top Blumotion, soft close, self closing.
  • Exposed sides: not finished (construction holes show); City says to order finished end panels for them separately.
  • Toe kick: 4" tall with a 3" recess, built into the case; toe skins are sold separately.
  • Factory finish (not quoted): from primer only (+$75 on the B3DR) to paint (+$229 to +$273.80 on the B3DR); glass cabinets cost more (+$275 to +$721.80 on the W2DGLASS; the W1DGLASS not read).
  • Warranty (terms): five-year limited, against defects in material and workmanship, to the original buyer, not transferable. A worn slide or hinge is replaced under a separate lifetime limited warranty. Not covered: warping or bowing of doors and panels over 24" wide or 80" tall (the 96" tall end panels), warp up to 1/4", drawer fronts over 42" wide, hidden faces, open joint lines. City may substitute hardware or plywood of similar or better quality without notice (for example Salice hinges for Blum).

Differences from the plan

  • Toe kick: City's standard is 4" tall, not 4.5". The 34.50" height is assumed to include it; ask.
  • Cooktop base: the B2DR1FFCOOKTOP is a false front over two drawers, the DB36-3's layout as planned.
  • Pantry: City has no four-door tall cabinet (the T2D has one door pair), so it is built as two (read 2026-11-02): a 36" × 54" × 24" T2D (two doors over four roll-outs, +$300 of the price), which City says is made to stack, with a 36" × 39" × 24" W2D (two doors over shelves) set on top, 93" in all, split at 54" like the planned pantry. The T2D's doors get a mid rail; how that works on a slab front isn't stated, so ask. Its roll-outs are rubberwood.
  • Oven column: City has no match for the OVD3393. The closest is the TAPPLIANCECASE (read 2026-11-04), priced at 33" × 84" × 24", the tallest offered: 9" short of the plan, with no back, doors or drawer, and an opening 31 1/2" wide and 78 1/2" tall. So the line is marked as a different size, and City's total is not like for like. A panel, doors and a way to close the top 9" would have to be sourced; ask City.
  • B21-FH: priced as a B1D, one door; the listing doesn't say whether its door runs the full height with no drawer rail, so ask. The wall cabinets over the Garage and the Toaster, Air Fryer and Coffee are one-door W1Ds and W1DGLASSes (read 2026-11-04).
  • Cabinet A: one drawer over one door, hinged on the left (assumed; same price either way).
  • Trash: the B0DPULLOUTTRASH is a full-height pull-out for two cans, 18" wide.
  • End panels (BFE, TFE): 3/4" slab, paint grade, raw; depths are 24 5/8" for the box plus the door. The panel on the oven column is 3/4" thick and takes that much from the Garage column beside it, so the B21-FH and uppers there would have to narrow to 20 1/4".
  • Over the fridge: the RW3721 is a W2D built 37" wide and 20 3/4" tall, the nearest 1/4" step to the plan's 20 11/16", with no fillers (read 2026-11-03).
  • Side walls: Coffee and the cabinets over it butt a stud wall, so they get no end panels (removed 2026-11-01).
  • Glass cabinets: a plain W1D with "Prep door for glass" (no charge listed) is $257 less per cabinet at sale, $1,028 over the four, and has the finished birch interior instead of a raw one. Not priced here.
  • Not priced: the FP2496 skin (City's thinnest end panel is 3/4"), toe skins, crown and the riser molding.
