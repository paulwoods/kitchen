Construction

What sets it apart: every cabinet is built to the planned size (widths in 1/4" steps), so nothing is swapped for a near match. The box is 3/4" formaldehyde-free plywood, as heavy as Access, on heavy-duty Blum hardware with drawers rated 100 lb. The fronts arrive raw, ready to paint on site, and exposed sides need separate end panels.

  • Type: frameless, full overlay (European style), ready to assemble. Construction holes show on the sides once assembled.
  • Box: 3/4" Columbia PureBond plywood (formaldehyde free, made in the USA from North American lumber), maple veneer inside with a UV-cured clear finish. Glass door cabinets have a raw veneer interior to match the fronts instead.
  • Back: not stated separately.
  • Doors and drawer fronts (as quoted): slab plywood, paint grade, vertical grain, not grain matched; sanded and raw, to be primed and painted on site. Standard drawer fronts: 5 7/8" top drawer, the lower drawers equal.
  • Glass doors: the glass cabinets do not offer slab fronts, so they are priced with the Shaker Inset Panel frame (paint grade alder). The glass is not included.
  • Shelves: not stated.
  • Drawer boxes: 5/8" hard white maple, dovetailed, 1/4" bottom captured on all four sides, 100 lb capacity, assembled and clearcoated (conversion varnish). The trash pull-out comes only with a 5/8" Baltic plywood box.
  • Drawer sizes inside: not published. The cooktop base lets you pick the drawer box depth (12" to 27"); 21" is priced, to clear the cooktop body.
  • Glides: Blum Blumotion heavy-duty undermount, soft close, 100 lb, made in the USA.
  • Hinges: Blum Clip Top heavy duty, soft close, fully adjustable, made in the USA.
  • Exposed sides: not finished (screw holes and the maple interior show); Barker says a finished end panel is always required on an exposed side, sold separately.
  • Toe kick: 4" tall with a 3" recess, built into the case; its face is bare plywood, covered with toe skins or base molding (sold separately).
  • Factory finish (not quoted): conversion varnish, from primer only (+$81 on the B3DRV3) to two coats of paint (+$190 to +$248 per cabinet).
  • Warranty (terms): five-year limited, against defects in material and workmanship, to the original buyer, not transferable. Worn slides and hinges are replaced for life ("lifetime limited"). Not covered: warping or bowing of doors and panels over 24" wide or 80" tall (the 95" tall end panels), drawer fronts over 42" wide, hidden faces, natural veneer marks, open joint lines. Barker may substitute similar hardware without notice (for example Salice hinges for Blum). Warranty requests by email only.

Barker's tall pantry box starts at 48 1/4", too tall for the upper part.
