Construction

What sets it apart: the same Cordova box, drawers and Blum hardware as Monaco, but a shaker door with solid maple stiles and rails. The only quote with solid wood in the doors. Exposed sides cost extra.

  • Type: frameless (full access), shipped assembled. All Cordova cabinets are made and assembled in the USA.
  • Box: 5/8" plywood sides, top and bottom, UV finished, natural maple interior with edgebanding to match.
  • Back: 1/4" UV finished birch, with 5/8" plywood hanging rails and stretchers (7/8" at the rails).
  • Doors and drawer fronts (Cosmopolitan): 5-piece "skinny shaker": 1 3/4" solid Northern hard rock maple stiles and rails (15/16" profile on the face), European mortise and round-end tenon joints, 3/16" MDF recessed center panel. Fog is a painted gray-green.
  • Shelves: 3/4" plywood, adjustable; half depth in base cabinets, full depth in wall cabinets.
  • Drawer boxes: 5/8" solid maple, English dovetail, 1/4" plywood bottom captured on all four sides.
  • Drawer sizes inside (cabinets.com interior drawer box sheet, frameless): 19 3/4" deep; 21 1/8" wide in a 24" base, 24 1/8" in a 27", 30 1/8" in a 33", 33 1/8" in a 36". In a 3-drawer base the top box is 3 1/4" tall and the two below are 5 1/4" (side heights as read off the sheet). The 4.3" spice jars do not stand upright in the top drawer.
  • Glides: Blum full-extension undermount with integrated soft close (Blum Motion).
  • Hinges: Blum, integrated soft close, 6-way cam adjustable.
  • Exposed sides: unfinished. "Matching Finished Sides" is a paid option on each cabinet, both sides only: 10% of a base cabinet's price, 15% of a wall cabinet's (read 2026-10-28). It is priced on the seven cabinets with an exposed side.
  • Toe kick and legs: not stated.
  • Certifications (site-wide): CARB 2 compliant, KCMA quality tested.
  • Warranty: not stated on the product or collection pages.
