Construction

What sets it apart: the second cheapest of the seven quotes (after Edgeline), with a lifetime limited warranty (the only other lifetime claim is Edgeline's). It is framed, like Edgeline and Madison, with a birch face frame and engineered (not solid wood) doors. The boxes go together with sliding dovetail joints, not metal clips. Back thickness and glide rating are not published.

  • Type: framed, full overlay, ready to assemble with a sliding dovetail joint (no L-brackets or visible clips). Ships in 1 to 21 business days from Adrian, MI or Clearwater, FL.
  • Face frame: Grade A birch rails and stiles.
  • Box: 1/2" birch plywood. Interior natural wood veneer, UV coated; exterior matches the door finish.
  • Doors and drawer fronts: shaker, flat panel, full engineered door; 5-piece engineered drawer fronts that match the doors. White paint.
  • Shelves: adjustable, 3/4 depth.
  • Drawer boxes: 5/8" solid wood, dovetailed.
  • Drawer sizes: not listed. The B24 has "butt doors" (a pair meeting in the middle, no center stile).
  • Glides: undermount, full extension, soft close (rating not given). The pantry roll-outs (RS30) come with their own glides.
  • Hinges: soft close, adjustable.
  • Exposed sides: match the door finish.
  • Toe kick: not stated as included; a 96" × 4 1/2" toe kick skin (TK8, $48.81) is sold separately.
  • Warranty: lifetime limited, on the cabinets, hinges and drawer glides, to the original buyer, residential only, in the USA, not transferable. Repair or replacement with an equivalent part, no labor or reinstallation. Not covered: wear, misuse, improper installation or storage, environmental conditions, natural wood color change. Warping under 1/4" is not a defect, and glass doors carry no guarantee against warping.

Differences from the plan

  • Painted sides (2026-10-28): exposed sides come finished to match the doors at no charge, so the spices (left side), Cabinet A (right side), Coffee and the W2730 and W2712 over Coffee (right side) need no option or panel.
  • Glass doors: the "BGFI" cabinets are glass door cabinets with a finished interior. The listing does not say whether the glass is included; check before ordering.
  • Pantry: no 36" wide pantry (utility cabinets come 18", 24" and 30" wide). The U308424 is 30" wide and 84" tall: two 29" doors on top, two 49 1/4" doors below, three adjustable shelves and one fixed. Three RS30 roll-out shelves are added for the lower part. A 30" × 12" × 12" glass cabinet (W3012BGFI) on top takes it to the 96" ceiling, in place of the W361224.
  • Sink base: the SB42 is 42" wide, as planned, but ships as a B42 (two doors, two drawers) that is converted to a sink base on site.
  • Microwave base: the MB30 is 30" wide (3" wider than the MWB27) with one drawer under an open 27" wide opening (the listing gives 20" and 17" for its height). Check the HMD8454UC fits it.
  • Air Fryer and Coffee: no 27" drawer base; the DB24-3 is 3" narrower.
  • With those widths the wall run is 183", 9" short of 192"; the island is 192", as planned. Fillers or wider neighbours make up the rest.
  • Trash: a B18FHD (full-height door) with a door-mounted double 35 qt waste pull-out (4WCBM18DM2, soft close; lids not available).
  • Oven base: a plain B33 (two doors, two drawers); the Colorado oven cabinets are 84" to 96" tall, not base height.
  • Cabinet A: the B24 has two butt doors under one drawer; there is no 24" single-door base.
  • Tall end panels: the REP1.596 is 1 1/2" × 96" × 24", the planned size.
  • Fillers come 3" wide, cut down on site to 1.5". The 42" fillers are cut to 34.5" and 42".
