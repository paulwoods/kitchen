Construction

What sets it apart: one of two framed lines of the six (with Madison), and the cheapest. The box is the thinnest (1/2" plywood) and the doors are all HDF, but exposed sides come finished at no charge. The face frame narrows each opening, so drawer boxes are smaller than in a frameless cabinet of the same width.

  • Type: framed, full overlay, ready to assemble (metal clip assembly). Assembled cabinets are an option with a 2-week lead time; RTA ships in 3 to 5 business days.
  • Face frame: 1 3/4" x 3/4" solid wood, mortise and tenon.
  • Box: 1/2" plywood, natural maple finish inside. Base corner brackets are metal or wood.
  • Back: not stated separately (part of the 1/2" plywood box).
  • Doors and drawer fronts (Edgeline): 5-piece HDF thin shaker, recessed panel, painted white. No solid wood in the fronts.
  • Shelves: 3/4" plywood, adjustable.
  • Drawer boxes: dovetail, 5/8" solid wood sides (species not stated), 3/8" plywood bottom.
  • Drawer sizes inside: not published.
  • Glides: full-extension undermount, concealed, soft close (brand not stated).
  • Hinges: concealed, 6-way adjustable, integrated soft close (brand not stated).
  • Exposed sides: finished to match, included.
  • Toe kick and legs: not stated.
  • Warranty: limited lifetime (the site FAQ lists Edgeline; its buyer's guide leaves Edgeline off that list, so confirm).

Differences from the plan

  • Glass doors: the "GD" cabinets come prepped for glass with a finished interior, but the glass is not included (priced from a glass shop separately).
  • Wall cabinets over the oven, Toaster, Air Fryer and Coffee: no 27" tall cabinets. Each W2727 + W2715 pair becomes a 30" tall cabinet at 54" with a 12" glass cabinet at 84" (same 42" total to the ceiling); likewise the W3327 + W3315 over the oven.
  • Pantry: no 36" wide or 81" tall cabinet. The U309624 is 30" wide and 96" tall (to the ceiling), two door pairs over shelves, no roll-out trays and no glass cabinet above it.
  • Sink base: no 42"; the SB36 is 36" wide. The 33 1/2" Ruvati sink fits a 36" base only if its 34 1/2" inside clears the bowl flanges; check before ordering.
  • Microwave base: the BMW30 is 30" wide (3" wider than the MWB27) with one drawer under the opening, as planned.
  • Drawer bases for Air Fryer and Coffee: no 27"; the 3DB24 is 24" wide (the 3DB30, $752.68 / $526.88, is the 30" option).
  • With those widths the wall run is 183", 9" short of 192"; the island is 186", 6" short. Fillers or wider neighbours make up the rest.
  • Trash: the B18TCPO is a single door with a drawer above and two 35-qt bins, not a full-height pull-out.
  • Oven base: a plain B33 (two doors, two drawers); the OW-B33-FH's oven opening is not offered at base height (only 84"+ oven cabinets).
  • Cabinet A: the B24 has double doors over one drawer; there is no 24" single-door base.
  • Tall end panels: the REPV3096 is 1 1/2" x 96" x 30" deep (a refrigerator panel), 6" deeper than the 24" TEP.
  • Fillers come 3" wide, cut down on site to 1.5". The 42" fillers are cut to 34.5" and 42".
