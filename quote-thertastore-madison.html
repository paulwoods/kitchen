Construction

What sets it apart: the second cheapest of the six quotes (after Edgeline), with a 20-year limited warranty and a full 1/2" plywood back. It is framed, like Edgeline, with a birch face frame and all-HDF doors. The drawer glides are rated 70 lb, the lowest of the quotes that give a rating, and the pantry roll-outs are not soft close.

  • Type: framed, full overlay, ready to assemble (metal clips, or glue and staple). Ships in 3 to 7 business days; pre-assembled cabinets are sold separately.
  • Face frame: 3/4" × 1 3/4" birch.
  • Box: 1/2" A-grade plywood sides, top and bottom, and a full 1/2" plywood back. 3/4" solid wood corner blocks in the base cabinets. UV coated natural interior.
  • Doors and drawer fronts: shaker, HDF frame (2 3/4" wide) and HDF center panel with a beveled edge; 5-piece drawer fronts. Navy, 7-step acid-catalyzed finish with a UV coat. No solid wood in the fronts.
  • Shelves: 3/4", adjustable, edge banded at the front; half depth in base cabinets.
  • Drawer boxes: 5/8" solid hardwood, dovetailed, 5/16" plywood bottom.
  • Drawer sizes: 3-drawer bases have a 6 1/4" top front and two 11 1/8" fronts below. Inside box sizes are not listed for them (the 2DB24's boxes are 20" W × 8 1/4" H × 20 3/8" D).
  • Glides: undermount, full extension, soft close, 70 lb. The trash pull-out uses Blumotion soft-close glides (110 lb). Roll-out trays (pantry) use side-mount ball-bearing glides, not soft close.
  • Hinges: concealed European, 6-way adjustable, soft close. Doors are pre-drilled; you pick left or right at assembly.
  • Exposed sides: finished. Wall cabinet bottoms are finished outside.
  • Toe kick: 1/2" plywood, included; an 8' toe kick cover (TKC) is needed where cabinets sit side by side.
  • Certifications: CARB compliant.
  • Warranty: 20-year limited, against defects in materials and workmanship, to the original buyer, residential only. Repair or replacement of the defective part (for example a door, not the whole cabinet), no labor. Not covered: humidity warping or splitting, natural wood changes, damage from handling or assembly. Parts may be unavailable if the line is discontinued. A paid 5-year protection plan is offered.

Differences from the plan

  • Wall cabinets over the oven, Toaster, Air Fryer and Coffee: no 27" tall cabinets. Each W2727 + W2715 pair becomes a 30" tall cabinet at 54" with a 12" glass cabinet at 84" (same 42" to the ceiling); likewise the W3327 + W3315 over the oven.
  • Pantry: no 36" wide pantry (only 18", 24" and 30"). The U3084244RS is 30" wide and 84" tall: two doors on top, two below, one fixed and two adjustable shelves and four roll-out trays (one more than planned). A 30" × 12" glass cabinet (W3012GD) on top takes it to the 96" ceiling, in place of the W3615.
  • Sink base: no 42"; the SB36 is 36" wide. The 33 1/2" Ruvati sink fits a 36" base only if the opening between the face frame stiles clears it; check before ordering.
  • Microwave base: the BMW30 is 30" wide (3" wider than the MWB27) with one 6 1/4" drawer under a 26 1/2" W × 16 1/2" H × 23" D opening, as planned. Check the HMD8454UC fits it.
  • With those widths the wall run is 189", 3" short of 192"; the island is 186", 6" short. Fillers or wider neighbours make up the rest.
  • Trash: the B18FHTCPO is a full-height door on a double trash pull-out with one lid, as planned.
  • Oven base: a plain B33 (two doors, two drawers); the Madison oven cabinets are 84" to 96" tall, not base height.
  • Cabinet A: the B24 has double doors over one drawer; there is no 24" single-door base.
  • Tall end panels: the REPV3096 is 1 1/2" × 96" × 30" deep (a refrigerator panel with a 1 1/2" stile, finished both sides), 6" deeper than the 24" TEP.
